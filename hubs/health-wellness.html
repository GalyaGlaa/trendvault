Daily Health Tips

Common Health Myths Debunked

  • Myth: "Carbs make you fat" → Truth: Excess calories cause weight gain, not specific macros
  • Myth: "You need 8 glasses of water daily" → Truth: Hydration needs vary by individual
  • Myth: "Supplements replace healthy eating" → Truth: Food first, supplements as backup
  • Myth: "Local honey prevents allergies" → Truth: No scientific evidence supports this claim
