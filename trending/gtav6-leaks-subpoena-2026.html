GTA 6 Leaks: Take-Two Issues Subpoenas Against Microsoft, Discord, GitHub

Published: August 21, 2026 • 4 min read • By TrendVault Editorial

Adsterra 728x90 Banner

Take-Two Interactive has filed federal subpoenas against Microsoft, Discord, and GitHub in response to massive leaks of unreleased footage and gameplay from Grand Theft Auto VI, confirming the authenticity of materials circulated by an anonymous group calling themselves Cyberleek.

The legal filings, made public this week, reveal that the leaked content includes authentic map images and gameplay footage from the highly anticipated game — marking one of the largest intellectual property breaches in gaming history.

What Was Leaked?

The Cyberleak group has released multiple pieces of allegedly stolen content over the past two weeks, including:

  • Map screenshots showing detailed in-game locations in Vice City
  • Gameplay footage featuring protagonist characters Jason and Lucia
  • Internal documents suggesting a playable demo version exists

The leaks have generated millions of views across social media platforms, with fans scrambling to analyze every frame for Easter eggs and release date clues.

Key Timeline

A week before the official Netflix premiere, Cyberleak began distributing leaked materials. Take-Two responded within 72 hours with legal action targeting platforms hosting the content.

Take-Two's Legal Response

The subpoenas seek to identify individuals responsible for stealing and distributing confidential Take-Two materials. Court documents indicate the company believes at least some of the leaked content originated from an insider or compromised development build.

"These actions represent serious violations of intellectual property rights and trade secret protections," the company stated in its filing. Take-Two is requesting user data from Discord and GitHub to trace the source of the leaks.

Microsoft, which owns GitHub, has reportedly cooperated with the investigation while respecting user privacy guidelines.

Impact on GTA 6 Release

Despite the leak chaos, industry analysts predict no impact on the planned release timeline. Rockstar Games is expected to showcase official footage on Netflix within days, potentially shifting focus from leaks to verified content.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick previously confirmed that GTA 6 will launch in Fall 2025 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, with PC ports following later.

Community Reaction

Gaming communities have reacted with mixed emotions:

  • Excitement: Fans celebrate getting early glimpses despite the controversy
  • Concern: Many worry about security implications for future Rockstar releases
  • Debate: Discussions continue about whether watching leaked content supports piracy
