Apple Lays Off 60 Vision Pro Employees as Company Pivots to Smart Glasses

Published: August 21, 2026 • 4 min read • By TrendVault Editorial

Adsterra 728x90 Banner

Apple has laid off at least 60 employees from its Apple Vision Group and Vision Pro division, marking a significant restructuring of the company's spatial computing efforts as it redirects resources toward smart glasses and artificial intelligence initiatives.

The layoffs, reported by Bloomberg and confirmed by multiple tech publications including MacRumors, 9to5Mac, and AppleInsider, represent a strategic shift in Apple's hardware priorities following the Vision Pro's lukewarm market reception since its February 2024 launch.

What We Know About the Layoffs

According to reliable industry sources, the job cuts affected:

  • Vision Pro development team members working on next-generation headset features
  • Immersive video and gaming teams focused on VR/AR content creation
  • Siri digital assistant staff as part of broader AI integration efforts

Apple confirmed the cuts to Bloomberg, stating the company is "looking to evolve our business to deliver the best experiences for our users." The company added that while new roles will be created, the changes will "impact a limited number of existing roles."

Key Facts

Minimum 60 Vision Pro team members laid off | Part of larger restructuring affecting Siri and gaming teams | Resources redirected toward smart glasses development

Betrayal or Strategic Pivot?

The layoffs come amid reports that Apple has suspended development of:

  • A second-generation Vision Pro headset with M5 chip improvements
  • A cheaper, lighter "Vision Air" model originally planned for 2027

Instead, Apple is reportedly accelerating work on smart glasses — a more wearable, affordable alternative to the $3,499 Vision Pro. Industry analysts suggest the company sees smart glasses as the future of wearable computing, especially after Meta's success with Ray-Ban smart glasses.

"Apple is betting on wearability and AI instead of bulky headsets," said one tech analyst familiar with the situation. "The $3,499 price point was simply unsustainable for mass adoption."

Timeline of Vision Pro Setbacks

  • February 2024: Vision Pro launches at $3,499 with mixed reviews
  • October 2025: Vision Pro refresh announced, but sales remain modest
  • May 2026: Reports surface that Apple is shifting Vision Pro team to smart glasses
  • August 2026: 60+ layoffs confirmed across Vision Products Group

What This Means for Consumers

Despite the layoffs, Apple continues releasing visionOS updates — the fifth beta of visionOS 27 arrived earlier this month. The company has not confirmed any plans to discontinue the Vision Pro, suggesting existing users will still receive software support.

However, the cuts signal that Apple no longer views the Vision Pro as a mass-market product in the near term. Smart glasses — expected to launch by late 2027 — may become Apple's flagship wearable device instead.

For developers building spatial computing apps, the news raises questions about long-term platform viability. Some are already diversifying their portfolios to include iOS and macOS alternatives.
